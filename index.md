# Century Arcade

Games and puzzles, preserved for their future.

## Crosswords

Maintained by Alex DeJarnatt.

- [The xd Crossword Corpus](https://centuryarcade.org/xdcorpus/): more than 160,000 published crosswords from over 160 publications, 1913 to today, kept in plain text for research. Access on request.
- [The Cross(word) Reference](https://crosswordreference.com): look up clues, answers and constructors across crossword history.
- [xd.saul.pw](https://xd.saul.pw): statistics and reports over the corpus, rebuilt nightly.
- [xdformat](https://github.com/century-arcade/xdformat): the .xd plain-text crossword format and converters.
- [Every Puzzle Project](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject): digitizing historical crosswords from scans.

## Games

Maintained by Saul Pwanson.

- [Below the Root](https://root.saul.pw): Saul's preservation of the 1984 Commodore 64 game.
